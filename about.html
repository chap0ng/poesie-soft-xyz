Creative Practice

Basile is a Swiss artist, composer, and interaction designer. His multidisciplinary practice sits at the intersection of interaction design, game design, illustration, music, and experimental storytelling. Through code, drawing, sound, objects, installation, and audiovisual forms, he develops dreamlike narrative ecosystems where interaction, listening, and collective imagination shape fragmented, non-linear stories. co-creator of Blue Ash Studio, currently working as a sound designer at Panflip Studio, and as an independent 2D/3D artist on various projects. Currently active under the aliases Chap0ng (illustration) and Oh Deer (music).
